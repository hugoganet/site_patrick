about us

We craft every story together.
At Warans, every project receives our full attention. We collaborate closely with each client to develop tailor-made solutions, grounded in precision, purpose, and thoughtfulness. Every form, every space, every detail is intentional—designed to resonate and leave a lasting impression.

Warans is a Paris-based agency founded in 2024 by Patrick Paleta, a creative director and consultant specializing in brand identities. After co-founding Chevalvert in 2007, Patrick launched Warans to pursue a singular vision: work that is deliberate, striking, and timeless.

Our methodical approach places the client and their audience at the heart of every project. Serving both institutional and commercial clients, warans delivers work that is radical in its simplicity, bold in its clarity, and enduring in its impact.
